ShutterSlim

Find visually similar photos in your Apple Photos library using on-device AI. Powered by Apple's Vision framework for semantic matching, it uncovers photos with the same scene from different angles or lighting—ones other cleaners miss. Works seamlessly with Photos.app. Fully local, no subscriptions.

Precise Semantic Matching

Goes beyond exact duplicates. Detects visual similarity based on content, not just pixels. Catch near-matches like vacation shots from varied perspectives or family gatherings under different lights.

On-Device Processing

Everything runs locally with Apple's Vision framework. No cloud uploads, no privacy risks. Fast scans on your Mac, respecting your library's scale.

Seamless Photos Integration

Direct access to Photos.app albums, including Recently Saved and Recently Deleted. Accurate names and thumbnails—no mismatches or flashes.

Smooth, Reliable Experience

Stable UI with no jitter during scans. Progress updates cleanly. Refined sidebar and album switching for effortless navigation.

How It Works

Step 1: Launch and Select

Open ShutterSlim. Choose an album or scan your entire Photos library directly from the app.

Step 2: AI-Powered Scan

On-device Vision framework analyzes images for semantic similarity. Progress tracks smoothly without interruptions.

Step 3: Review Matches

Browse grouped similars with precise thumbnails. Zoom, compare details side-by-side.

Step 4: Clean Up

Select and delete duplicates or similars. Changes sync instantly back to Photos.app.
